The Kiln Ministries

Text Message (SMS) Program

How we use SMS  ·  Consent  ·  Opt-out  ·  Privacy
Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Section 1

About Our Messaging

The Kiln Ministries, a nonprofit organization registered in Tennessee, sends text messages (SMS) to support two of its ministry programs. We send only program-related notifications. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages, and we do not use text messaging for advertising.

No mobile information (including your phone number) is sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Section 2

Our Two Messaging Programs

Come to Me — Counselor Notifications

Come to Me (cometome.ai) is The Kiln Ministries' online counseling outreach. Approved ministry counselors receive an SMS notification when a new visitor begins a conversation, so that a counselor can log in and respond. These messages go only to recruited ministry staff.

Great Physician — Counselee Homework & Session Reminders

Great Physician (thegreatphysician.ai) is The Kiln Ministries' biblical-counseling app. A counselor may send a counselee their assigned homework or a session reminder by text. These messages are initiated by the counselee's own counselor as part of their counseling relationship.

Section 3

How You Consent (Opt-In)

We obtain consent before sending text messages. There is no automated web opt-in form; consent is given directly to a person within the ministry:

  • Come to Me counselors are personally recruited by ministry leadership and explicitly agree — verbally and in a written record kept by the ministry — to receive SMS notifications as part of their counseling role.
  • Great Physician counselees provide their own mobile number to their counselor and agree to receive homework and session reminders from that counselor. A counselee may decline at any time and continue counseling by other means.

Message frequency varies based on counseling activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Section 4

Opting Out & Getting Help

Reply STOP Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further texts.
Reply HELP Reply HELP for assistance, or email support@thekiln.org.

If you opt out, you can opt back in at any time by letting your counselor or ministry contact know.

Section 5

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are used solely to deliver the program notifications described above. No mobile information (including your phone number) is sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. Our full data practices are described in our Privacy Policy.

Your phone number is shared only with Twilio, the messaging provider we use to transmit these text messages on our behalf; it is not used for any other purpose.
