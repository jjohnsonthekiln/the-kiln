Section 2

Text Message (SMS) Program Terms

The Kiln Ministries offers text message notifications in support of two ministry programs:

  • Come to Me — approved ministry counselors receive an SMS notification when a new visitor begins a conversation, so a counselor can respond.
  • Great Physician — a counselor may send a counselee their assigned homework or a session reminder by text.

Consent

We send text messages only to recipients who have agreed to receive them. Consent is obtained directly through the ministry — Come to Me counselors are recruited and explicitly agree to receive notifications, and Great Physician counselees provide their mobile number to their counselor and agree to receive homework and session reminders. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Message Frequency & Cost

Message frequency varies based on counseling activity. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. The Kiln Ministries does not charge for these messages.

Opting Out and Help

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message; you will receive a single confirmation and no further texts. Reply HELP for assistance, or email support@thekiln.org.
